## 🌟 What Can PingPin Do?

PingPin is your personal assistant for managing hybrid work schedules (WFO / WFH). It handles daily attendance tracking, company HR portal check-ins, missed day recovery, and commute planning so you don't have to worry about missing attendance targets.

---

## 🔥 Features

### 📡 1. Automated Wi-Fi Attendance Tracking
- **Automatic Office Detection**: Logs your attendance on your calendar automatically as soon as your phone connects to your office Wi-Fi network.
- **On-Time vs. Late Marking**: Automatically classifies check-ins as **Present** or **Late** based on your configured shift start time.
- **Smart 2:00 PM Afternoon Check**: Verifies afternoon office arrivals to prevent false missed-day notifications.

### 🌐 2. HR Portal Auto-Punch & Auto-Fill
- **Auto-Login & Auto-Punch**: Automatically fills in your username/password and clicks the Check-In or Check-Out button on your company HR web portal.
- **Floating Overlay Window**: Perform check-ins inside a convenient floating mini-window while using other apps.
- **1-Tap Alarm Actions**: Perform check-ins or check-outs directly from punctual alarm screens.

### 🔄 3. Smart "Makeup WFO" Rescheduler
- **Missed Day Recovery**: Detects missed WFO days and proposes replacement dates on upcoming WFH days.
- **Target Tracking**: Suggestions automatically cancel as soon as you fulfill your required weekly office attendance targets.

### 🌦️ 4. Commute Weather & Travel Advisories
- **Live Commute Weather**: Displays hourly weather for key travel times (8 AM, 9 AM, 5 PM, 6 PM, 8 PM).
- **Rain & Heat Alerts**: Sends advisories for severe rain or high heat so you can plan whether to take a cab or metro.

### 📅 5. Multi-Year Indian Holiday Directory
- **36 States & UTs Filter**: Filter holidays specifically by your state or region across 2024–2036.
- **Long Weekend Badges**: Automatically highlights holidays falling on Monday or Friday with a `LONG WEEKEND` badge.
- **Relative Countdowns**: See instant tags like "Today!", "Tomorrow!", or "In 3 days".

### ⏰ 6. Punctual Alarm Alerts & Quick Leave Request
- **Guaranteed Alarm Alerts**: Punctual alarm alerts with non-stop ringtones that fire reliably even when your phone is in deep sleep mode.
- **Slide-to-Confirm Controls**: Slide up to Check-In, Check-Out, or Snooze (5m, 10m, 15m, 30m).
- **Apply for Leave Today**: Send a leave application email with 1 tap directly from the alarm screen.

### 📊 7. Calendar History & Monthly Compliance
- **Weekly & Monthly Views**: Toggle between a 7-day expandable strip and a full monthly calendar view.
- **Compliance Gauge & Streaks**: View your monthly WFO target percentage gauge and track consecutive attendance streaks.
- **PDF Report Exporter**: Generate and share formal monthly attendance statements and daily log tables as PDF files.

### 🚀 8. In-App Updates & Custom Themes
- **GitHub Auto-Updater**: Receive notifications for new releases with 1-tap in-app updates.
- **Light & Dark Themes**: Switch between crisp E-Ink light mode and charcoal dark mode.
